# GCA-UCR-Milestones
<h1>What is GCA-UCR-Milestones?</h1>
<p>This repository holds all my major assignments from my Data Analytics course from UCR's Global Career Accelerator. These milestones show my progress in Excel functions, written analysis, visualizations in Excel, pivot tables, and A/B testing using Excel.</p>

<p>Below you'll see each Milestone and its descriptions.</p>

<h2>Milestone 1: YouTube</h2>
<p>Analyzed 6,000+ trending YouTube videos to identify key performance drivers, enabling data-driven recommendations to boost content reach and engagement.</p>

<h2>Milestone 2: Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART)</h2>
<p>Analyzed BART ridership records to uncover weekday commuting trends and high-volume Yellow Line usage, informing service optimization and infrastructure planning.</p>

<h2>Milestone 3: DoorDash</h2>
<p>Analyzed delivery data across major U.S. markets to uncover trends in delivery time, order volume, and cuisine preferences, driving region-specific strategies to improve efficiency and customer satisfaction.</p>

<h2>Milestone 4: Excel Visualizations of Winter Sports</h2>
<p>Analyzed and visualized Google Trends data for nine Olympic sports to uncover cyclical patterns in public interest, identifying key event-driven spikes and providing insights to guide content strategy and seasonal marketing.</p>

<h2>Milestone 5: A/B Testing at H&M</h2>
<p>Conducted and analyzed an A/B test on promotional email subject lines and provided data-driven recommendations to improve customer engagement during a seasonal marketing campaign.</p>

<h2>Milestone 6: Terracotta</h2>
<p>Analyzed customer survey data using XLOOKUP and PivotTables; identified top products, key purchase factors, and data-driven strategies to boost online sales.</p>

<h2>Final Portfolio Project: The Recording Academy</h2>
<p>Utilized advanced Excel analytics, PivotTables, conditional logic (IF, SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS), and XLOOKUP, to calculate user engagement KPIs and mobile visitor rates, comparing performance of Grammy.com and RecordingAcademy.com to assess the business impact of their 2022 website split.</p>
